About

A little bit of code.
A lot of curiosity.

I've never been good at picking one lane. I topped my district in Class 12, then spent the same years painting, dancing, hosting programs and entering every quiz I could find.

Now I'm a BTech student at NXTwave, learning web development, Python and Generative AI. What I like most is the moment an idea stops being a plan and becomes something that actually runs in a browser.

I learn by building. I'm early in this — most of what I know is beginner-level, and I'd rather say that plainly than dress it up. But I'm consistent, I ask a lot of questions, and I finish what I start.

Outside code, I'm the person who'll volunteer to hold the mic. Anchoring and public speaking taught me something engineering rarely does: an idea is only as good as your ability to make someone else care about it.
